01 About

A one-person, read-only research prototype.

Bizel Research observes public Reddit discussions, assesses their relevance, and prepares draft replies for human review. The researcher reviews and edits every draft, and manually posts any contribution from their own Reddit account.

The study measures genuine organic engagement over time, using publicly visible scores and comment activity. The application itself never posts.

02 Features

Built to observe, score, and draft—never to post.

  • Public Reddit reader

    Searches Reddit’s public JSON endpoints for relevant discussions. Requests are paced at about one per second, with backoff when rate limits appear.

  • Predicted opportunity scoring

    Each discussion receives a 0–100 score, clearly labeled as a prediction. It reflects keyword relevance and basic intent signals, not a guaranteed outcome.

  • Grok reply drafts

    Prepares draft replies for human review and editing. If a draft model is unavailable, a simple template is used instead. A person decides whether anything is posted.

  • Local draft saving

    Drafts and score history are stored locally on the researcher’s computer, not on a remote server.

03 Safety & ethics

Designed around restraint.

  • Never posts, comments, votes, or sends messages automatically.
  • Never buys, coordinates, or manipulates votes.
  • Does not use multiple accounts.
  • Read-only and low volume, with paced public requests.
  • Keeps only minimal data, which is deleted after the study.

Observation communities

Initial observation focuses on these communities. Discussions are observed first, and each community’s rules are followed.

  • r/SaaS
  • r/smallbusiness
  • r/Entrepreneur
